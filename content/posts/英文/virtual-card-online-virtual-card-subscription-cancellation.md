Follow the merchant's cancellation process, save confirmation, and check for any pending or final billing activity.

A simple recordkeeping routine helps you understand card activity and follow up on subscriptions. Verify the merchant and transaction details before taking action.

## Review the details

Check the transaction date, amount, currency, merchant descriptor, and status. For subscriptions, note the cancellation date, final billing period, and any confirmation reference.

## Cancellation checklist

1. Use the merchant's official account or support channel.
2. Follow the stated cancellation steps.
3. Save the cancellation confirmation and date.
4. Check whether a final or pending transaction remains.
5. Keep records securely and protect card credentials.

If a charge appears after cancellation, compare the transaction date with the cancellation confirmation and the merchant's billing terms. Contact the merchant for subscription questions and the card provider for transaction-status concerns.

For information about CBPayments, visit [https://www.cbpayments.cc/](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and review current product details, fees, availability, and terms.

## FAQ

### What proves a subscription was cancelled?
Keep the confirmation email or account status, cancellation date, and any support case number.

### Should I retry if the cancellation page errors?
Check the account status and contact the merchant before assuming the cancellation failed.

## Final takeaway

Use the official cancellation process, save proof, and review subsequent transactions against the merchant's terms.
