# Virtual Cards for Online Appointment Booking and Service Deposits

Online appointment platforms may collect booking fees or deposits for consultations, lessons, salons, and other services. A virtual card may be accepted by some providers, but acceptance and cancellation terms differ by merchant, region, and card programme. Explore [CBPayments](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) for virtual card information and verify the service provider's current payment rules before booking.

## Read the Deposit and Cancellation Policy

Check whether the payment is a deposit, a booking fee, or the full service price. Review cancellation deadlines, no-show rules, and whether the deposit is refundable or transferable.

## Confirm the Provider and Appointment Details

Verify the provider's identity, date, time zone, location, and service. Keep the confirmation number and any messages about rescheduling.

## Check Card Acceptance and Authorisation

Some providers may authorise a card before the appointment or charge a deposit immediately. Ensure the card supports the transaction and has sufficient available balance.

## Rescheduling and Refunds

If you need to cancel, use the official booking system and save confirmation. If a refund is approved, processing time depends on the merchant and card programme.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I pay a booking deposit with a virtual card?

Possibly, depending on the service provider and card programme.

### Will cancelling the appointment automatically refund the deposit?

Not always. The provider's cancellation terms determine the outcome.

### What if I was charged but have no booking confirmation?

Contact the provider before paying again and check whether the transaction is pending or completed.

## Final Thoughts

Before paying, verify merchant acceptance, the total amount, billing frequency, cancellation and refund terms, and the card programme's current rules. Keep receipts and confirmation records, and use the merchant's official support channel when a transaction is unclear.
