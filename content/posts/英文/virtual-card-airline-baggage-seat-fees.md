Airlines may sell checked baggage, seat selection, priority boarding, and other extras separately from the ticket. A virtual card may work for these online purchases when accepted, but airline rules and card eligibility vary.

For a general overview of virtual-card options, visit [CBPayments](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and review the available product terms before choosing a payment method.

## Confirm the final itinerary cost
Check baggage allowance, dimensions, weight limits, seat terms, and whether an extra applies to each flight segment. Some add-ons are non-refundable or have restrictions after check-in. Confirm the passenger name and booking reference before paying.

## Pay through official channels
Use the airline’s official website or app where possible. If using a virtual card, ensure the card is active and has enough available balance for the full amount, including any displayed taxes. Save the updated itinerary and payment receipt. If an add-on is purchased through a third-party agency, its change and refund process may differ from the airline’s.

## Troubleshoot a failed add-on purchase
Check the booking status before retrying. A failed checkout can sometimes leave an authorization pending even if the add-on was not confirmed. Verify card details and contact the airline or booking provider through official support if the payment status is uncertain.

## FAQ
**Can I use a virtual card to buy baggage?** It depends on airline payment acceptance and card restrictions.

**Will an add-on always be refundable?** No; check the fare and ancillary-service terms.

**Where can I review card options?** Visit [CBPayments](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and confirm compatibility before checkout.

## Final thoughts
Keep add-on receipts with your itinerary and verify that every paid service appears in the booking.
