A card payment can appear as pending before it becomes a final posted transaction. Understanding the difference between authorisation and settlement helps explain temporary balance reductions, changed totals, and why a refund may not appear immediately.

## What happens during authorisation

Authorisation checks whether a transaction can proceed and may reserve part of the available balance. Settlement is the later process in which the merchant submits the final transaction for collection. The timing depends on the merchant, payment processor, card network, and issuer.

## Why settlement can show a different amount

Some transactions begin with an estimated amount, such as orders with adjustments or services that use deposits. The final settled amount can differ when the merchant completes the transaction, subject to applicable rules. A reversal may release a hold rather than create a separate refund entry.

## How to investigate a payment that stays pending

Check the transaction status and the merchant’s stated processing window. Keep the receipt and order reference. If the hold remains longer than expected or the posted amount is wrong, contact the merchant and then the card issuer with the transaction details.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will every website accept a virtual card?

No. Acceptance depends on the merchant, card network, issuing country, card type, and the provider’s rules. Check the merchant’s payment policy before relying on a card.

### What should I do if a payment is declined?

Check the available balance, card status, expiry date, billing details, transaction limits, and any authentication request. If the cause is unclear, contact the merchant or issuer through official support.

### Is a virtual card a guarantee against fraud?

No payment method eliminates all risk. Use trusted websites, protect verification codes, monitor transactions, and contact the issuer promptly if you suspect unauthorised activity.

## Final Thoughts

A virtual card is most useful when its acceptance, limits, fees, and billing rules match the purchase you need to make. Check the merchant’s requirements first, keep your receipts, and use official support channels if a payment or refund does not behave as expected.
