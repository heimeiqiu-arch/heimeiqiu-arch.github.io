Review credit packages, image resolution tiers, expiry rules, and usage logs to understand the cost of AI image generation.

AI image and video services may charge by generated output, resolution, duration, or prepaid credits. Before buying, identify the billing unit and understand how settings and usage limits affect the final cost.

## Check the plan

Read the provider's official pricing page. Confirm the currency, credit expiry, included allowance, resolution or duration limits, queue options, and refund rules. Save the plan details and purchase confirmation.

## Keep usage visible

1. Record the project using the service.
2. Review credit balances and usage logs.
3. Check quality settings before large jobs.
4. Set spending reminders or alerts where available.
5. Compare the invoice or credit purchase with the account history.

If the final cost differs from expectations, look for higher resolution, longer output, additional attempts, or usage beyond the included allowance. Ask the provider for a breakdown and verify transaction status before retrying.

Protect account credentials and payment details. Merchant acceptance varies, so check supported methods before relying on a virtual card for a time-sensitive purchase.

## FAQ

### Why might credits run out sooner than expected?
Output quality, duration, retries, or additional features may consume more credits than a basic job.

### What should I retain?
Keep receipts, credit purchase references, usage logs, plan terms, and notes about settings changes.

## Final takeaway

Understand what consumes credits, monitor usage, and retain transaction records to make AI generation costs easier to explain.
