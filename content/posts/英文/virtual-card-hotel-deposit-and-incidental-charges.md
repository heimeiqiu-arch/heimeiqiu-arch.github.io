Online payment requirements vary by merchant, country, transaction type, and card provider. Before entering card details, check the terms that apply to your specific booking or purchase. The steps below help you reduce avoidable errors and keep a useful record if something goes wrong.

## Before you pay

Read the merchant's payment, cancellation, and refund policies. Confirm the total price, billing currency, expected charge date, and whether a deposit or later adjustment may apply. If the merchant lists restrictions on prepaid, debit, or virtual cards, treat those restrictions as important rather than assuming the checkout will accept every card.

## Step-by-step checks

1. **Use the official website or app.** Navigate to the merchant directly instead of following an unexpected payment link.
2. **Review all entered details.** Check the card information, billing address, account name, and any order-specific fields.
3. **Complete verification carefully.** If an authentication prompt appears, confirm that it belongs to the payment you initiated. Never share a one-time code with a person who contacts you.
4. **Wait for a clear result.** Save the confirmation page or reference number. If the page freezes, check the account and order status before trying again.
5. **Keep a record.** Save the receipt and the merchant's terms, especially for travel bookings, preorders, deposits, or recurring services.

## If the payment does not work

A decline can occur even when the card details are correct. Possible causes include merchant restrictions, transaction limits, balance requirements, billing-detail mismatches, issuer risk checks, or a failed authentication step. Review any error message, then consult the card provider's official guidance. Avoid repeated rapid attempts, which may create multiple pending authorisations or trigger further security checks.

## Protect your account and information

Use a secure device and updated browser, and avoid entering payment information on shared or public computers. Do not send complete card numbers, security codes, passwords, or authentication codes in ordinary support messages. When contacting support, provide only the details needed to locate the transaction and use the provider's official contact route.

If you are comparing online payment options, you can review CBPayments at [https://www.cbpayments.cc/](https://www.cbpayments.cc/). Check current product availability, fees, usage restrictions, and terms before relying on any card for a particular merchant.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does successful verification mean the payment is approved?
No. Authentication helps verify the payer, while the merchant and issuer still need to authorise the transaction.

### Should I retry after a checkout error?
First check whether the order or transaction was created. If the status is unclear, contact the merchant or provider before submitting another payment.

### What should I include in a support request?
Include the transaction date and time, amount and currency, merchant name, current status, relevant error message, and order reference if available. Do not include your full card credentials or one-time codes.

## Final takeaway

Confirm the merchant's rules, verify the details, complete security checks only through trusted flows, and keep the receipt. If an online payment remains unclear, verify its status before retrying and use official support to resolve it.
