Check active subscriptions, renewal dates, price changes, and payment method status to manage recurring charges.

A consistent checklist makes online payments easier to verify and helps reduce avoidable errors. Before paying, confirm the merchant, total amount, currency, card limits, and any verification requirements.

## Before checkout

Use the merchant's official website and review the product or service terms. Confirm the final price, taxes, shipping, renewal conditions, and whether your card type is accepted. Check the provider's current limits and available balance.

## Keep a record

1. Save the order confirmation and receipt.
2. Record the merchant, date, amount, and currency.
3. Note the order reference and transaction status.
4. For subscriptions, record the renewal date and cancellation deadline.
5. Store records securely without copying full card credentials into ordinary spreadsheets.

## If something goes wrong

Check the exact error and transaction status before retrying. Contact the merchant for order or invoice questions and the card provider for authorisation or account issues. Never share one-time codes or full card credentials with someone who contacts you unexpectedly.

## FAQ

### Does a correct card detail guarantee approval?
No. Merchant acceptance, transaction limits, verification requirements, and provider checks can also affect approval.

### What should be reviewed for recurring payments?
Check the service is still needed, the next renewal date, any price change, and the cancellation process.

## Final takeaway

Confirm the terms before paying, keep a clear transaction record, and verify the status before retrying a failed attempt.
