Track recurring merchants, payment dates, business owners, and cancellation instructions to reduce forgotten subscriptions.

A repeatable expense process helps a team understand what each online purchase was for, who approved it, and whether the final amount matches the invoice. A virtual card is only one part of that recordkeeping process.

## Fields worth tracking

For each recurring service or purchase, record the merchant, business purpose, owner, cost centre, amount, currency, billing frequency, renewal date, invoice reference, and transaction status. Store the information securely and limit access to people who need it.

## Monthly review checklist

1. Collect invoices and receipts.
2. Match each transaction to its supporting record.
3. Confirm the business purpose and approval.
4. Investigate duplicate, unfamiliar, or mismatched charges.
5. Record refunds, credits, and unresolved items for follow-up.

## Keep the process practical

Use consistent categories and naming conventions. Assign an owner to each subscription and set reminders before renewals. If a transaction is pending, verify its status before treating it as final or repeating a payment.

Protect card details and avoid storing full credentials in expense spreadsheets. Use official merchant and provider support channels when information does not match.

## FAQ

### What makes a useful expense record?
A clear business purpose, invoice or receipt, date, amount, currency, transaction reference, and approval information.

### How often should recurring charges be reviewed?
A monthly reconciliation and a separate review before renewal are useful starting points for many teams.

## Final takeaway

Use consistent fields, collect supporting evidence, and review recurring charges regularly. Good records make it easier to explain and manage online spending.
