Check visit allowances, participating airports, guest fees, renewal terms, and cancellation rules before subscribing to an airport lounge service.

Travel add-ons and activities may have separate prices, strict deadlines, and limited refund options. Review the details carefully before paying so you know which service you are purchasing and what happens if your itinerary changes.

## Before checkout

Use the airline, operator, or booking provider's official website. Confirm the passenger or participant details, route or location, service date, currency, restrictions, cancellation terms, and any extra charges. If the booking is time-sensitive, save the confirmation immediately after checkout.

## Payment checklist

1. Verify the provider and exact add-on.
2. Check whether the fee applies per person, per segment, or per booking.
3. Read refund and change conditions.
4. Save the receipt and booking reference.
5. Compare the final transaction with the amount displayed at checkout.

## If the booking changes

Review the provider's rules before paying for a replacement or modification. If checkout fails, verify whether the original booking and transaction were created before retrying. Contact the travel provider for itinerary or refund questions and your card provider for transaction-status concerns.

Protect card credentials and authentication codes. Some travel merchants may impose specific card requirements, so check the supported methods before relying on a virtual card.

You can review CBPayments at [https://www.cbpayments.cc/](https://www.cbpayments.cc/). Check current fees, product details, availability, and terms before selecting a payment method.

## FAQ

### Are travel add-ons always refundable?
No. Refundability depends on the provider's policy, the type of add-on, and when a change is requested.

### What should I save?
Keep the itinerary, add-on details, receipt, booking reference, policy terms, and any messages about changes or refunds.

## Final takeaway

Confirm exactly what the fee covers, understand the restrictions, and keep your booking records. This makes it easier to resolve a discrepancy or request support later.
