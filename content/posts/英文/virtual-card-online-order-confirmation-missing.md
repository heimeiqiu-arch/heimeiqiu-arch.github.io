Check order history, spam folders, and transaction status before placing another order when a confirmation email is missing.

Small checkout details can cause confusion even when a payment method is otherwise valid. Check the merchant's instructions and transaction status before changing details or submitting another attempt.

## Review the checkout information

Confirm the merchant's official domain, card details, billing address, currency code, final amount, and any order-specific fields. Browser autofill may insert old information, and some forms require a particular name or address format. Follow the merchant's documented guidance rather than guessing.

## A practical checklist

1. Review the exact field or error message.
2. Check saved autofill data and correct outdated details.
3. Confirm the currency code and total price.
4. Check whether an order or authorisation already exists.
5. Save the receipt or order reference after a successful payment.

## If the problem continues

Do not retry repeatedly if the order status is uncertain. Contact the merchant for checkout or order questions and the card provider for transaction-status concerns. Share only the information needed to identify the transaction; never send full card credentials or one-time verification codes in ordinary messages.

Virtual card acceptance varies by merchant and provider, so confirm supported payment methods before relying on one for an urgent purchase.

## FAQ

### Why can correct details still be rejected?
The merchant may use specific formatting rules or additional verification requirements. Check its help page and the exact error message.

### What if the confirmation email is missing?
Check order history and transaction status before paying again, then contact the merchant if the order remains unclear.

## Final takeaway

Check the details carefully, verify the order status, and use official support rather than repeatedly retrying checkout.
