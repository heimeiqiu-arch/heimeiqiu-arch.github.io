Understand expired checkout sessions, saved cart status, and safe steps to confirm payment status before retrying.

A checkout issue can be caused by the merchant, payment processor, card issuer, or the connection between them. Verify what happened before submitting another payment.

## Start with transaction status

Check the merchant's order history and your card account. Look for a confirmation number, pending authorisation, posted charge, or decline message. An expired session does not prove that no transaction was created.

## Troubleshooting steps

1. Confirm you are using the merchant's official checkout.
2. Review the exact error message and timestamp.
3. Check the order and transaction status.
4. Follow the merchant or card provider's official guidance.
5. Save receipts, order references, and support case numbers.

## Avoid repeated attempts

Multiple rapid retries may create additional authorisations or duplicate orders. If status is unclear, contact the merchant to confirm the order and contact your card provider for issuer-side questions. Merchant restrictions may include card type, region, billing details, or recurring-payment rules.

Protect card credentials and verification codes. Virtual card acceptance varies by merchant and provider, so confirm supported methods before relying on a card for an urgent purchase.

For information about CBPayments, visit [https://www.cbpayments.cc/](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and review current terms, fees, availability, and product details.

## FAQ

### Does a decline always mean no order was created?
Not necessarily. Check the order and transaction records to confirm whether an authorisation or order exists.

### What should I include in a support request?
Provide the time, amount, currency, merchant, error message, and order reference if available. Do not share full card details or authentication codes.

## Final takeaway

Verify transaction status and follow official guidance before retrying a payment.
