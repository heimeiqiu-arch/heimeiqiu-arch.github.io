Travel insurance is often purchased online as a one-time payment, although some policies or memberships may renew. A virtual card may be accepted by an insurer or comparison site, but policy eligibility and payment acceptance must be checked separately.

For a general overview of virtual-card options, visit [CBPayments](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and review the available product terms before choosing a payment method.

## Verify the policy first
Read the policy wording, covered destinations, trip dates, exclusions, excess, emergency contact details, and cancellation rules before paying. Make sure the insured traveller information is accurate. A successful card payment does not prove that the policy covers a particular activity or pre-existing condition.

## Choose a suitable payment method
For a one-time premium, confirm the card supports online purchases and the transaction amount. If the product renews automatically, make sure the card can be reused and remains valid for the renewal. Save the policy certificate, receipt, and insurer contact information in an accessible place.

## If payment does not complete
Check whether the insurer accepted the application, whether the card was charged or merely authorized, and whether an email confirmation was issued. Do not submit repeated payments without checking transaction status, as duplicate authorizations can occur. Contact the insurer through its official channel if the status is unclear.

## FAQ
**Can I buy travel insurance with a virtual card?** Possibly, depending on the insurer and card rules.

**Does a payment confirmation mean coverage has started?** Check the policy documents for the effective time and conditions.

**Where can I explore virtual-card services?** Visit [CBPayments](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and verify acceptance with the insurer before purchase.

## Final thoughts
Review coverage before checkout and keep policy documents—not just the card receipt.
