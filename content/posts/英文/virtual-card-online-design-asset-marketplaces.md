# Using a Virtual Card on Design Asset Marketplaces

Design asset marketplaces sell fonts, templates, icons, stock graphics, mock-ups, and other downloadable resources. Some accept eligible virtual cards, while others apply restrictions by card type, region, or payment processor. If you are comparing virtual card options for digital purchases, [CBPayments](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) is one place to research; confirm the current terms and marketplace acceptance before paying.

## Review the Licence, Not Just the Price

Check whether the asset licence covers personal, commercial, client, or redistribution use. A download may be limited to a specific number of projects or users. Save the licence terms with the receipt.

## Confirm Checkout and Currency

Check the marketplace's accepted payment methods, total price, currency, and any taxes. If conversion applies, the final amount may differ from an online currency estimate.

## Handle Failed Downloads Carefully

If a charge appears but the file is unavailable, check your account and email before paying again. Contact the seller or marketplace with the order reference and a screenshot of the error.

## Refunds and Support

Digital download refunds depend on the marketplace's policy, the product, and applicable law. Ask support how technical failures, duplicate orders, or incorrect files are handled.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I buy stock graphics with a virtual card?

Possibly, if the marketplace accepts the card type and region.

### Can I use purchased assets commercially?

Only if the licence permits the intended use; read the terms before buying.

### What if a download is missing after payment?

Check the order status and contact the marketplace before retrying the payment.

## Final Thoughts

Before paying, verify merchant acceptance, the total amount, billing frequency, cancellation and refund terms, and the card programme's current rules. Keep receipts and confirmation records, and use the merchant's official support channel when a transaction is unclear.
