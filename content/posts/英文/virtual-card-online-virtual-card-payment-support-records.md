Gather the transaction timestamp, amount, currency, merchant, status, and reference number before contacting support.

A repeatable review process can help you manage online payments with fewer surprises. Check the provider's terms and the merchant's requirements before relying on a virtual card for a particular transaction.

## What to review

Confirm the merchant, total amount, currency, billing frequency, card limits, and any verification requirements. For recurring services, record the renewal date and cancellation deadline. Compare provider fee schedules rather than assuming every card has the same cost structure.

## Keep useful records

1. Save receipts and order references.
2. Record the transaction date, amount, and currency.
3. Keep a calendar of renewals and cancellation dates.
4. Review transaction history for unfamiliar or duplicate activity.
5. Store support case numbers and correspondence securely.

## If you need help

Check transaction status before retrying a payment. When contacting support, provide the date, amount, currency, merchant, status, and reference number if available. Never send full card credentials or one-time authentication codes in ordinary messages.

For information about CBPayments, visit [https://www.cbpayments.cc/](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and review current product details, fees, availability, and terms before deciding whether it fits your needs.

## FAQ

### What should I compare between providers?
Review transaction and currency fees, limits, restrictions, refund handling, and available support channels.

### Why use a renewal calendar?
It helps make recurring costs visible and gives the responsible person time to review or cancel a service before billing.

## Final takeaway

Check the terms, maintain clear records, and review recurring payments regularly to keep online spending organised.
