Virtual card products can differ in fees, transaction limits, eligibility, and merchant acceptance. Reviewing the provider's current terms before use helps set realistic expectations for a particular purchase.

## What to check

Read the official terms and fee schedule. Confirm transaction and balance limits, supported currencies, card usage restrictions, refund handling, and available support channels. Check whether specific merchants or recurring payment types may be restricted.

## Compare providers carefully

1. Review all published fees, not only the headline price.
2. Check limits and any eligibility requirements.
3. Confirm how refunds and pending transactions are handled.
4. Understand the official support and dispute process.
5. Keep a copy of the terms that applied when you signed up.

## If a payment fails

Compare the available balance with the transaction limit and the final purchase amount. Review any error message and check whether an authorisation or order was created. When contacting support, provide the date, amount, currency, merchant, status, and reference number, but never share full card credentials or one-time codes.

## FAQ

### Does a sufficient balance guarantee payment approval?
No. Merchant acceptance, transaction limits, verification requirements, and issuer checks may also affect authorisation.

### What should I compare in a fee schedule?
Check transaction fees, currency conversion, issuance, inactivity, and any other charges that apply to your expected usage.

## Final takeaway

Review the actual terms, fees, limits, and restrictions before relying on a virtual card for a specific online payment.
