Review the provider's notice, effective date, plan details, and cancellation options when a recurring service changes its price.

Online purchases can involve separate authorisation, settlement, tax, and billing steps. Understanding the record shown by the merchant and the card provider helps explain why the final amount or date may differ from the initial checkout.

## Compare the records

Save the original checkout total, merchant receipt, invoice, transaction currency, posted amount, and any fee disclosure. For digital services, note the billing address or tax information supplied to the merchant and the period covered by the invoice.

## What to check

1. Confirm the merchant and service period.
2. Compare the original and final amounts.
3. Check currency conversion and any disclosed fees.
4. Review tax line items and price-change notices.
5. Contact the merchant or card provider if the difference remains unexplained.

## Avoid assumptions

A refund can be processed at a different exchange rate from the original purchase, and a posted transaction can differ from a pending authorisation. Subscription price changes may also have a notice period or cancellation deadline. Review the provider's current terms rather than assuming every adjustment is an error.

Protect payment credentials and use official support channels. Keep receipts and correspondence securely.

## FAQ

### Why can the posted amount differ from checkout?
Currency conversion, tax, merchant adjustments, or the difference between authorisation and settlement may affect the final amount.

### What records should I keep?
Keep receipts, invoices, transaction references, currency details, and any price-change or refund notices.

## Final takeaway

Compare the invoice, receipt, and posted transaction before deciding a charge is incorrect. Clear records make international billing easier to understand.
