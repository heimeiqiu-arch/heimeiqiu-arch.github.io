# Hotel and Car Rental Card Deposits: Holds, Charges, and Refunds Explained

When checking into a hotel or collecting a rental car, you may be asked to provide a payment card even if you have already paid for the booking.

The merchant may place a temporary authorisation hold to cover potential additional costs, such as room service, fuel, tolls, damage, or other charges permitted by the agreement.

This can be confusing because the amount may reduce your available balance without appearing as a completed purchase. Knowing how deposits work can help you choose a suitable card and avoid unexpected payment problems.

## What Is a Hotel or Car Rental Card Deposit?

A card deposit can take different forms, depending on the merchant and its payment arrangements.

One common method is a **pre-authorisation**, which checks whether sufficient funds or credit are available and reserves an amount for a potential future charge.

The reserved amount can reduce your available balance even though the merchant has not yet collected it as a completed payment.

Other merchants may collect a deposit directly or use a different arrangement. Always ask whether the amount is a temporary authorisation or an actual charge.

## Why Do Hotels Place Holds on Cards?

Hotels may request an authorisation to cover possible expenses during a stay.

Depending on the hotel's policy, this may include:

- Room service or food and beverages
- Paid amenities
- Incidental expenses
- Potential additional nights or other agreed charges

The amount and release procedure vary by property, booking terms, card type, and local practices.

A hotel may also require a card at check-in even if the room was prepaid. Prepayment for accommodation does not necessarily cover incidental expenses.

## Why Do Car Rental Companies Require Deposits?

Car rental providers may use a card authorisation to cover potential costs that are not fully known when the vehicle is collected.

These may include fuel differences, tolls, additional rental time, or charges arising under the rental agreement.

Deposit requirements can vary significantly according to the rental company, vehicle category, insurance or protection package, location, and payment method.

Before booking, read the rental terms and confirm the deposit amount, accepted card types, and conditions for releasing the hold.

## How Does a Pre-Authorisation Affect Your Available Balance?

Imagine your card has an available balance of 500 units in its billing currency. A hotel places a temporary authorisation for 150 units.

Your available balance may fall to approximately 350 units while the authorisation remains in place. The exact display depends on the issuer and the account's other transactions.

If the final eligible hotel charges total 90 units, the merchant may submit the completed transaction for that amount and release the unused portion of the authorisation. The timing and amount of the release depend on how the transaction is processed.

The key point is that **available balance and posted balance are not always the same**. A temporary hold can restrict funds even before a final charge appears.

## How Long Does a Hotel or Rental Deposit Take to Be Released?

There is no single release time that applies to every card and merchant.

The timing may depend on:

- When the merchant submits the final transaction or releases the authorisation
- The merchant's payment processor
- The card issuer's policies
- Weekends, holidays, and processing delays
- Whether the transaction is completed, reversed, or left pending

Ask the merchant when it will release the authorisation. If the hold remains longer than the merchant's stated timeframe, contact your card issuer with the transaction date, amount, and merchant details.

A pending authorisation is not necessarily a refund. If an amount was actually charged and later returned, that is a different process.

## Can You Use a Virtual Card for a Hotel or Rental Car?

Sometimes, but acceptance should never be assumed.

A merchant may distinguish between cards used for online booking and cards presented at check-in or vehicle collection. It may also have requirements about the cardholder's name, card type, physical card presentation, deposits, or additional verification.

Before relying on a virtual card, ask the hotel or rental provider:

1. Does the property or branch accept this specific type of virtual card?
2. Can it be used for the deposit, or only for advance payment?
3. Must the same card be presented at check-in or collection?
4. What is the required deposit amount?
5. Can the card support the expected authorisation and any final adjustment?

If the merchant requires a physical card, a virtual card may not meet its policy even if it worked for the initial online booking.

For card options and features, visit [CBPayments](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and verify the applicable terms before booking. Acceptance ultimately depends on the merchant and the card's issuing arrangements.

## How to Avoid Deposit-Related Payment Problems

### Confirm the deposit before booking

Ask for the deposit amount, payment method requirements, and release conditions. Do not rely solely on the advertised room or rental price.

### Keep enough available balance

The deposit may be separate from the booking amount. Make sure your card can accommodate both the expected purchase and the temporary hold.

### Check the accepted card type

A merchant's policy may differ between online payments, in-person payments, and deposits. Confirm the exact requirements with the property or rental branch.

### Keep your receipts and booking documents

Save the booking confirmation, deposit terms, check-in receipt, and final invoice. These records help explain the difference between a hold and a completed charge.

### Check your statement after checkout

Review whether the authorisation was released and whether the final transaction matches your invoice. If both a hold and a completed charge remain visible, check their statuses before assuming you have been charged twice.

### Contact the right party

Ask the merchant to confirm when it released the authorisation. If the hold remains on your card, ask the issuer whether it is still pending and what information is needed to investigate it.

## What If a Deposit Is Charged Instead of Held?

Some merchants may collect an actual deposit rather than placing a temporary authorisation. If so, the return of the money may be processed as a refund.

Refund timing differs from authorisation-release timing. Check the merchant's written policy and ask whether the original entry was a completed charge or a temporary hold.

If the merchant confirms that a refund was issued but it does not appear within the stated timeframe, contact your card issuer and provide the relevant transaction information.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a hotel hold more than the room price?

Yes. Depending on the property's policy and booking terms, the authorisation may include an amount for incidental expenses in addition to accommodation.

### Does a pending deposit mean the merchant has taken the money?

Not necessarily. A pending authorisation can reduce the available balance without being a completed charge. Check the transaction status with your issuer.

### Why has my deposit not been released after checkout?

The merchant may not yet have released the authorisation, or the issuer may still be processing it. Contact the merchant first, then ask the issuer to investigate if needed.

### Can I avoid a deposit by paying in advance?

Not always. Prepayment may cover the main booking while the merchant still requires a separate authorisation for incidental costs or other obligations.

## Final Thoughts

Hotel and car rental deposits are often used to manage costs that cannot be finalised at the start of a stay or rental. The most important details are whether the deposit is a hold or a completed charge, how much available balance it requires, and when it should be released.

Confirm the policy before booking, use a payment method that meets the merchant's requirements, and keep your transaction records until the final amount is settled.
