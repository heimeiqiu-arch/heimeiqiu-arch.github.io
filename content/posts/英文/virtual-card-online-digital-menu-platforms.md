Digital menu and QR ordering platforms may charge per location, menu, device, or ordering feature. A virtual card can help track subscription fees when the vendor accepts it.

For a general overview of virtual-card options, visit [CBPayments](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and review the available product terms before choosing a payment method.

## Compare features and charges
Check menu limits, language options, allergen labels, QR ordering, payment integration, and analytics. Some platforms charge separately for online orders or payment processing. Confirm what happens to menus and customer data if a subscription is cancelled.

## Manage locations and renewals
Record each restaurant or venue, account owner, plan tier, and renewal date. Use a reusable card for recurring billing where supported. Remove closed locations and unused add-ons before renewal, and keep a copy of menu data and key settings.

## Payment troubleshooting
Check card status, available limit, billing details, and vendor acceptance. If a charge is pending but the account remains inactive, review the order status before paying again.

## FAQ
**Can a virtual card pay for a QR menu service?** It depends on the platform and issuer rules.

**Does paying for QR ordering include transaction processing?** Not always; check the pricing terms.

**Where can I review virtual-card options?** Visit [CBPayments](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and confirm the vendor accepts the card.

## Final thoughts
Check location pricing and data export terms before relying on a digital menu platform.
