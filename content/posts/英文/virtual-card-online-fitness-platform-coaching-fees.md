Confirm coaching package scope, session limits, scheduling rules, and recurring charges before buying a digital fitness plan.

Online bookings and app subscriptions may have different payment schedules, cancellation rules, and renewal prices. Before paying, verify the service and make sure the terms match what you expect to receive.

## Before checkout

Use the provider's official website or app. Check the total cost, billing currency, service dates, trial end date if applicable, renewal frequency, and cancellation or refund policy. For appointments, confirm the time zone and any deposit requirements.

## A useful checklist

1. Confirm the provider and the exact service.
2. Check whether the charge is a deposit, package, or recurring subscription.
3. Save the booking confirmation and terms.
4. Record the renewal or appointment date.
5. Compare the final transaction with the receipt.

## If you need to cancel or change plans

Read the provider's cancellation window before making changes. If checkout appears to fail, verify whether the booking or transaction was created before trying again. Contact the provider for booking, access, and invoice questions; contact your card provider for transaction-status concerns.

Protect card details and authentication codes. Payment method acceptance varies by provider, so check supported options before relying on a virtual card for a time-sensitive appointment.

## FAQ

### What is the most important detail to check for a deposit?
Confirm what the deposit covers, when it becomes refundable, and what cancellation deadline applies.

### How can I prevent an app renewal from surprising me?
Record the renewal date and review the cancellation process before the next billing cycle.

## Final takeaway

Verify the provider, understand the billing and cancellation terms, and save the confirmation. A few checks can prevent avoidable confusion later.
