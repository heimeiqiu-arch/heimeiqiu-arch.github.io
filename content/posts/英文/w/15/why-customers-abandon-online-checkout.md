Customers may add products to a cart with every intention of buying, then leave before paying. This is checkout abandonment. Common causes include unexpected costs, lengthy forms, missing payment methods, unclear error messages, and low trust. Businesses should measure each stage of the purchase journey, identify where customers leave, and fix the biggest source of friction first.

## Unexpected costs
Show taxes, delivery charges, and mandatory fees as early as possible. Display the full amount before payment and explain how it is calculated.

## Complicated forms
Ask only for information needed to complete the order or meet applicable requirements. Consider guest checkout where appropriate and preserve non-sensitive information after validation errors.

## Trust and payment choices
Keep checkout branding consistent, identify the merchant, make support and policies accessible, and display only payment methods that are actually supported for the transaction.

## Payment errors
Explain the next step using the provider's actual response. If the result is uncertain, check transaction status before asking the customer to retry. Prevent repeated submissions while a payment is processing.

## Measure the journey
Track cart activity, checkout completion, payment authorization, failures by reason, and results by device and region. Combine analytics with support requests and transaction logs.

## Final thoughts
Clear pricing, simple forms, relevant payment options, and useful error messages make checkout easier to complete. Businesses can explore supported virtual and physical card options through [CBPayments](https://www.cbpayments.cc/).
