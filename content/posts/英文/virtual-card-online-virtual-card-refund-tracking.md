Record the original transaction, merchant refund confirmation, expected timeframe, and provider transaction status while waiting for a refund.

A simple recordkeeping routine helps you understand card activity and follow up on refunds, subscriptions, and international purchases. Always verify the merchant and the transaction details before taking action.

## Review the details

Check the transaction date, amount, currency, merchant descriptor, and status. Match the activity to the order confirmation or invoice. For refunds, keep the merchant's confirmation and expected processing timeframe. For subscriptions, note the cancellation date and any final billing period.

## Checklist

1. Save receipts and order references.
2. Review recurring charges and upcoming renewals.
3. Match refunds and credits to the original purchase.
4. Investigate unfamiliar activity through official support.
5. Keep records securely and protect card credentials.

## Avoid duplicate activity

If a transaction or refund is pending, do not assume it has failed. Check the merchant order history and provider status before retrying or paying again. Contact the merchant for order questions and the card provider for account or transaction concerns.

For information about CBPayments, visit [https://www.cbpayments.cc/](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and review current product details, fees, availability, and terms.

## FAQ

### What information helps track a refund?
Keep the original transaction reference, amount, currency, merchant refund confirmation, and the provider's current status.

### How should a subscription cancellation be documented?
Save the cancellation confirmation, date, case number if available, and any information about the final billing period.

## Final takeaway

Keep a clear transaction history, save proof of cancellation or refund, and use official support channels when activity remains unclear.
