Font marketplaces and typography tools may charge for individual font licences, team plans, or recurring design subscriptions. A virtual card can help track these costs when accepted by the provider.

For a general overview of virtual-card options, visit [CBPayments](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and review the available product terms before choosing a payment method.

## Check where the font can be used
Font licences may distinguish desktop, web, app, e-book, broadcast, or server use. Confirm page-view limits, user counts, client transfer rules, and whether a separate licence is needed for commercial projects. A payment receipt alone may not describe every permitted use.

## Keep purchases traceable
Record the font family, foundry, licence type, project, and invoice. If using a subscription library, check what happens to active projects and downloaded files after cancellation. Use a reusable card for recurring plans where supported and review renewals before the next billing date.

## Payment troubleshooting
Check card status, available balance, billing details, and merchant acceptance. If the licence email does not arrive after payment, check the account and spam folder before paying again.

## FAQ
**Can a virtual card pay for fonts?** Some font sellers accept virtual cards; check checkout rules.

**Does one font licence cover every use?** Not necessarily. Read the licence carefully.

**Where can I explore card options?** Visit [CBPayments](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and confirm compatibility with the seller.

## Final thoughts
Choose the licence for the intended use first, then keep its payment record with the project.
