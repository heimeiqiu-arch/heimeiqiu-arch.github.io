# What Is Reauthorization in Card Payments?

Some purchases cannot be completed immediately after the original card authorization.

A hotel stay may last longer than expected. A rental agreement may be extended. An online order may be shipped in several stages.

When the original authorization is no longer sufficient or has expired, the merchant may need to request a new approval.

This process is known as **reauthorization**.

## What Is Reauthorization?

Reauthorization is a new authorization request made for a transaction that already had an earlier authorization.

It may be needed when the original authorization is approaching its validity limit, has expired, or no longer reflects the amount required for the transaction.

A reauthorization is not an automatic extension of the original approval. It is a new request that must be evaluated under the applicable issuer, network, and processor rules.

## Why Do Merchants Reauthorize Payments?

### 1. Extended Hotel Stays

A guest may extend a hotel stay beyond the period covered by the original authorization.

The hotel may need to request another authorization to cover the additional expected amount, subject to its payment arrangement and applicable rules.

### 2. Longer Vehicle Rentals

A rental period may be extended or the final rental amount may change.

The merchant may need a new authorization to ensure that the payment arrangement remains valid.

### 3. Delayed or Split Shipments

An online retailer may not ship every item at the same time.

If fulfilment occurs after the original authorization validity period, the merchant may need to obtain a new authorization before completing the relevant payment.

### 4. Other Extended Services

Cruises, travel services, and other businesses with extended fulfilment periods may have specific authorization procedures.

The availability of reauthorization depends on the transaction type and applicable network rules.

## How Does Reauthorization Work?

A simplified flow is:

1. The merchant checks the status and validity of the original authorization.
2. It determines whether a new authorization is necessary.
3. The merchant submits a new request using the appropriate transaction information.
4. The issuer evaluates the new request.
5. The merchant receives an approval or decline response.
6. If approved, the merchant proceeds according to the applicable capture and clearing process.

The new authorization may be for the same amount or a different amount, depending on the transaction and rules.

## Reauthorization vs. Authorization Adjustment

These terms should not be confused.

An authorization adjustment changes an existing authorization amount where the relevant payment framework supports it.

Reauthorization is a new authorization request.

For example, a hotel may be able to adjust an existing authorization during a stay under an approved process. In another situation, the original authorization may have expired and a new request may be needed.

The available method depends on the network, transaction type, and provider capabilities.

## Reauthorization vs. Payment Retry

A payment retry is generally an attempt to process a payment again after an unsuccessful attempt.

Reauthorization is a new authorization request associated with a transaction that already had an earlier authorization.

The distinction matters because the merchant may need to identify whether the problem is an expired approval, a declined request, or a technical processing failure.

## What Should Merchants Consider?

Before reauthorizing, merchants should:

- Confirm the original authorization status.
- Check the applicable validity period.
- Verify that the transaction remains valid.
- Use the correct transaction references and indicators.
- Confirm the new amount is justified.
- Avoid creating duplicate payment attempts.
- Communicate with the customer when a new authorization may appear.

A new authorization can be declined even when the original one was approved.

## Potential Customer Impact

A new authorization may appear as another pending transaction.

If the merchant does not properly manage the original authorization, customers may temporarily see more than one reserved amount.

Clear transaction records and appropriate reversal procedures help reduce confusion.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does reauthorization guarantee payment?

No. It is a new authorization request and can be approved or declined.

### Is reauthorization the same as capturing a payment again?

No. Reauthorization requests a new approval. Capture is the process of completing an approved transaction for processing.

### Can a merchant reauthorize without contacting the customer?

The answer depends on the transaction agreement, stored-credential framework, network rules, and applicable law. Merchants should not assume that a new authorization is always permitted.

Reauthorization helps merchants manage extended or delayed transactions, but it must be used within the applicable payment rules and customer agreement.
