## Compare the billing model before checkout

Check employee-seat definitions, language availability, course library, reporting, and simulated campaign limits. Review how staff data is stored and whether integrations with identity systems cost extra. Ensure training content fits your organisation’s risk profile.

## Troubleshoot a declined payment methodically

If a payment is declined, check balance, limits, billing information, recurring-payment support, and authentication. Confirm vendor acceptance and account region. For higher invoices, compare employee counts and campaign usage with the selected plan.

## Keep recurring costs under control

Review active employees and training completion before renewal. Keep invoices with security costs and export reports for internal tracking. Before cancellation, download records you need and remove integrations safely.
