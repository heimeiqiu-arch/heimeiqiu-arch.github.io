Check trial end dates, plan prices, included features, and cancellation steps before a business software trial converts to a paid plan.

A few checks before checkout can prevent avoidable payment problems and make later reconciliation easier. Consider the full cost, applicable limits, and terms rather than focusing only on the displayed base price.

## Before paying

Confirm the merchant, item or service, total amount, currency, tax, shipping, recurring fees, and cancellation terms. For larger purchases, check the card provider's current limits and any merchant-specific payment requirements.

## Keep a simple record

1. Save the order confirmation and receipt.
2. Record the amount, currency, date, and merchant.
3. Note any renewal date or trial conversion.
4. Keep refund or cancellation confirmations.
5. Retain documents according to your business or personal recordkeeping requirements.

## Review changes over time

For recurring services, check price notices, plan changes, and revised cancellation terms before renewal. If a transaction fails, verify order status before trying again. Store payment records securely and do not include full card credentials or authentication codes in spreadsheets.

Use official merchant and card-provider support channels when information does not match. You can review CBPayments at [https://www.cbpayments.cc/](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and check current product details, fees, availability, and terms.

## FAQ

### What should be included in a purchase budget?
Include the full order total, tax, shipping, currency conversion where relevant, and any recurring charges you expect.

### How long should records be kept?
Requirements vary by jurisdiction and purpose. Follow applicable tax, accounting, and dispute requirements and your organisation's retention policy.

## Final takeaway

Check the full cost and applicable limits before paying, then retain the supporting records securely.
