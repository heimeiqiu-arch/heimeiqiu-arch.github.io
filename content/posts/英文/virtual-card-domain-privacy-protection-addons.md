Domain registrars may sell privacy protection, DNS security, monitoring, and other add-ons alongside domain registration. These services can renew separately from the domain itself, making it useful to track each charge clearly.

For a general overview of virtual-card options, visit [CBPayments](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and review the available product terms before choosing a payment method.

## Review what renews
Read the checkout summary to see which features are included, which are optional, and how each is billed. Domain privacy availability and eligibility vary by registry and registrar. Record separate renewal dates for the domain and any add-on so that cancelling one does not accidentally affect the other.

## Manage payment details
If the add-on renews automatically, choose a reusable card when supported. Keep enough available balance or limit for the expected renewal and any applicable taxes. Save invoices and name the expense by domain or project, especially if you manage several websites.

## Avoid common mistakes
Do not confuse domain privacy with website hosting, SSL certificates, or domain ownership protection. Before changing payment details, confirm which account and domain the card is attached to. If a renewal charge looks unfamiliar, compare the merchant descriptor with the registrar’s invoice and contact official support.

## FAQ
**Can a virtual card pay for domain privacy?** It may, if the registrar accepts the card.

**Will cancelling the card cancel the add-on?** No; cancel the add-on with the registrar and confirm the effective date.

**Where can I explore virtual-card options?** Visit [CBPayments](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and check eligibility for the intended merchant.

## Final thoughts
Treat domain add-ons as separate subscriptions and document what each recurring charge protects.
