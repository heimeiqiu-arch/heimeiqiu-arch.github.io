A digital marketplace purchase can involve different sellers, delivery methods, and refund policies. Before paying, confirm exactly what is being sold and how the marketplace handles disputes, cancellations, and refunds.

## Check the listing and seller

Use the marketplace's official website or app. Review the seller profile, product description, licence or usage restrictions, delivery method, and refund terms. Be cautious if a seller asks you to leave the marketplace's normal checkout process or share sensitive payment information through messages.

## Before you submit payment

1. Confirm the product, edition, quantity, and account or region requirements.
2. Review the full price, currency, taxes, and any marketplace service fee.
3. Check the seller's delivery estimate and refund policy.
4. Save the listing, order reference, and confirmation.
5. Verify that the payment method is supported by the marketplace.

## If an order is delayed or incorrect

Check the order page and delivery instructions before buying again. If the product is missing or different from the listing, open a support request through the marketplace and include the order reference and relevant evidence. If the transaction status is unclear, check with your card provider before repeating the payment.

Protect your card details, passwords, and one-time verification codes. A virtual card may be useful for some online purchases, but acceptance varies by merchant and card provider. Check current card terms, available balance, transaction limits, and verification requirements.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I pay a seller outside the marketplace?
Avoid moving outside the marketplace's official payment flow unless you have independently verified that the arrangement is legitimate and understand the protections you may lose.

### What records should I keep?
Save the product listing, seller details, receipt, order reference, delivery confirmation, and any support correspondence.

### What if a payment appears to have failed?
Check whether the order or transaction was created before trying again, to reduce the risk of duplicate payments.

## Final takeaway

Verify the seller and listing, understand the refund terms, use official checkout, and keep clear records. These habits make digital marketplace purchases easier to track and help you resolve problems if they arise.
