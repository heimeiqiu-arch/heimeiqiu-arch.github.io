Verify the contractor, written quote, payment milestones, and refund terms before submitting a deposit online.

Online service bookings may require an advance payment, deposit, or fee that is subject to specific cancellation conditions. Verify the provider and the booking details before entering payment information.

## What to confirm

Check the official provider page for the address or service location, date and time, included work or access, total price, currency, cancellation window, and refund rules. For home services, make sure the written scope and quote match what you requested. For reservations, review arrival windows and restrictions.

## Keep the booking organised

1. Save the quote or reservation page.
2. Confirm whether the payment is a deposit or the full amount.
3. Record the service date and cancellation deadline.
4. Keep the receipt, booking reference, and provider contact details.
5. Compare the final transaction with the agreed price.

## If you need to change the booking

Read the cancellation terms before paying an additional fee. If the payment page times out, check whether a booking or transaction already exists before trying again. Contact the provider about service scope or reservation details and your card provider about transaction-status issues.

Use official checkout pages and keep payment credentials private. Not every merchant accepts every virtual card, so confirm supported payment methods before relying on one for a time-sensitive reservation.

You can review CBPayments at [https://www.cbpayments.cc/](https://www.cbpayments.cc/). Check the current product details, fees, availability, and terms before choosing a payment method.

## FAQ

### Are online booking deposits always refundable?
No. Refundability depends on the provider's written terms and the timing of cancellation.

### What should I retain?
Keep the booking confirmation, itemised quote, receipt, cancellation terms, and any messages confirming changes.

## Final takeaway

Verify the provider and booking details, understand deposit rules, and retain proof of payment. These steps help you handle changes and resolve discrepancies more efficiently.
