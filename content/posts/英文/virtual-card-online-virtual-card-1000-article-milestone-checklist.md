A concise final review of merchant acceptance, transaction limits, billing terms, and recordkeeping for virtual card payments.

Before making an online purchase, confirm that the merchant accepts your card type and review the final amount, currency, billing details, and any recurring terms. Provider rules and merchant acceptance can vary.

## Final checks before checkout

1. Verify that you are on the merchant's official website.
2. Review the total cost, including applicable tax, shipping, and currency conversion.
3. Check the available balance, transaction limits, and verification requirements.
4. Read refund, cancellation, and renewal terms.
5. Save the receipt, order reference, and transaction status.

## After payment

Check that the order confirmation matches the purchase and keep supporting records securely. If the status is pending or unclear, verify whether the original transaction exists before retrying. Contact the merchant for order questions and the card provider for account or transaction issues.

Never share full card credentials or one-time authentication codes in ordinary support messages.

You can review CBPayments at [https://www.cbpayments.cc/](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and check current product information, fees, availability, and terms before deciding whether it suits your needs.

## FAQ

### Does a virtual card work at every online merchant?
No. Acceptance depends on merchant rules, card provider terms, transaction type, and verification requirements.

### What records should I keep?
Keep the receipt, invoice, order reference, transaction date, amount, currency, and relevant support correspondence.

## Final takeaway

Confirm the merchant and terms, check the full amount, and keep a clear record of each payment.
