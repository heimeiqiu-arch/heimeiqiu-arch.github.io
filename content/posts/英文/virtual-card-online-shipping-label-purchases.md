Online shipping platforms may charge per label, offer postage wallets, or bill business accounts periodically. A virtual card can help separate postage expenses from other purchases, as long as the platform accepts the card and the payment flow suits your shipping volume.

For a general overview of virtual-card options, visit [CBPayments](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and review the available product terms before choosing a payment method.

## Check how postage is billed
Some services charge each label immediately; others maintain a balance or issue periodic invoices. Understand refund rules for voided labels, address-correction fees, insurance, and surcharges. Do not assume a cancelled label will produce an instant refund to the card.

## Keep shipment records connected
For a shop or small business, record the order number, carrier, label cost, and refund status alongside the card transaction. If multiple employees buy postage, assign a clear process for approving purchases and reconciling unused labels. A dedicated card can make transactions easier to filter but cannot replace shipment-level records.

## If a label purchase fails
Verify the total amount, card status, available balance, billing address, and the platform’s accepted payment methods. Check whether the platform requires an account balance or business verification. Use official support if a charge appears but no label is issued.

## FAQ
**Can I buy shipping labels with a virtual card?** Some shipping platforms accept virtual cards; check their payment policy.

**Where does a label refund go?** It generally follows the platform’s stated refund process and may return to the original payment method.

**Where can I review card options?** Visit [CBPayments](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and confirm the service’s payment requirements.

## Final thoughts
Connect card records to order and carrier records so postage costs and refunds remain auditable.
