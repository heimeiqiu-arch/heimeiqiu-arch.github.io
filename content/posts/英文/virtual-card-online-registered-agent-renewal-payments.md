Record service periods, state-specific deadlines, renewal costs, and confirmation documents for registered agent services.

Professional services and compliance tools may combine a base fee with add-ons, filing charges, or recurring renewals. Before checkout, confirm exactly what the price covers and which deadlines or terms apply to your situation.

## Review the quote

Use the official provider website and read the current scope, service period, cancellation rules, refund policy, and any optional extras. Check whether a listed price excludes filing fees, taxes, expedited handling, or future renewals. Save the quote or order page with your records.

## Payment and recordkeeping checklist

1. Confirm the provider's identity and service scope.
2. Identify one-off fees and recurring charges separately.
3. Record deadlines and renewal dates.
4. Save the invoice, receipt, and service reference.
5. Reconcile the transaction with the amount agreed.

## If the invoice changes

Compare the final bill with the quote and look for additional filings, service upgrades, taxes, or currency conversion. Ask the provider to explain unexpected line items. If the payment status is unclear, check for an existing order before trying again.

Protect payment details and one-time verification codes. Acceptance varies by merchant, so confirm that your selected payment method is supported before relying on it for a deadline-sensitive service.

You can review CBPayments at [https://www.cbpayments.cc/](https://www.cbpayments.cc/). Check current terms, fees, availability, and product details before deciding whether to use it for a specific payment.

## FAQ

### Should add-on fees be checked before paying?
Yes. Optional services and expedited handling can change the total, so review the itemised quote.

### What records are useful later?
Keep the quote, invoice, receipt, filing or service reference, deadline details, and any support correspondence.

## Final takeaway

Check scope, add-ons, and deadlines before paying. Save the supporting documents and reconcile the final charge so you can resolve discrepancies efficiently.
