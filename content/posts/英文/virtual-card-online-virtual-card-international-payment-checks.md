Confirm merchant acceptance, currency, billing details, fees, and transaction limits before an international online purchase.

A clear payment routine starts with checking the merchant and understanding the transaction status. Virtual card features and merchant acceptance vary, so review the provider's current terms before making a purchase.

## Check before paying

Confirm the official merchant website, total amount, currency, billing details, recurring terms, and card limits. Save the order confirmation and understand the merchant's refund and cancellation rules.

## Keep the transaction record

1. Save the receipt or invoice.
2. Record the date, amount, currency, merchant, and order reference.
3. Check whether the transaction is pending, posted, reversed, or declined.
4. Match the transaction with the invoice.
5. For refunds, keep the merchant confirmation and expected timeframe.

## If the status is unclear

Do not repeat a payment until you have checked whether the original order or authorisation exists. Contact the merchant for order and refund questions, and the card provider for transaction-status or account issues. Keep card credentials and one-time codes private.

You can review CBPayments at [https://www.cbpayments.cc/](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and check current fees, availability, product information, and terms before choosing a payment method.

## FAQ

### Does a pending transaction mean the payment is final?
Not necessarily. Review the provider's status explanation and wait for the transaction to update if appropriate.

### What information helps with support?
Provide the transaction date, amount, currency, merchant, status, and reference number, without sharing full card details.

## Final takeaway

Verify the merchant and transaction state, keep clear records, and use official support when the details do not match.
