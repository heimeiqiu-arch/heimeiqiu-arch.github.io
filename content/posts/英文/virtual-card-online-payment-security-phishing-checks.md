A virtual card can limit the exposure of a primary card number, but it cannot make a fake checkout page or phishing message safe. The best protection is to verify the merchant and payment page before entering any card information.

## Check the website before entering payment details

Open the merchant’s site directly or use its official app instead of following an unexpected payment link. Check the domain carefully, look for unusual spelling, and be cautious of urgent messages claiming an order will be cancelled unless you pay immediately. A padlock icon alone does not prove a site is legitimate.

## Recognise common payment phishing tactics

Never share a one-time passcode, full card credentials, or account password with a person who contacts you unexpectedly. Be wary of fake refund pages, QR codes, support accounts, and attachments that ask you to sign in. If something seems wrong, stop and verify through an independent official channel.

## Respond quickly if card details may be exposed

If you entered card details on a suspicious page, contact the issuer promptly, freeze or replace the card if available, and review recent transactions. Change any reused password and secure the email account associated with your payment profile.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will every website accept a virtual card?

No. Acceptance depends on the merchant, card network, issuing country, card type, and the provider’s rules. Check the merchant’s payment policy before relying on a card.

### What should I do if a payment is declined?

Check the available balance, card status, expiry date, billing details, transaction limits, and any authentication request. If the cause is unclear, contact the merchant or issuer through official support.

### Is a virtual card a guarantee against fraud?

No payment method eliminates all risk. Use trusted websites, protect verification codes, monitor transactions, and contact the issuer promptly if you suspect unauthorised activity.

## Final Thoughts

A virtual card is most useful when its acceptance, limits, fees, and billing rules match the purchase you need to make. Check the merchant’s requirements first, keep your receipts, and use official support channels if a payment or refund does not behave as expected.
