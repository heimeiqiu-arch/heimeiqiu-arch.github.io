Track relevant transaction dates, merchant responses, supporting documents, and provider instructions when handling a card dispute.

Refunds and payment disputes can involve several stages, and the timing depends on the merchant, payment provider, and transaction type. Good records make it easier to understand the status and follow the correct process.

## Save the key evidence

Keep the order confirmation, receipt, transaction date, amount, currency, merchant descriptor, cancellation proof, and relevant correspondence. Store records securely and avoid including full card details or authentication codes in ordinary messages.

## Track the process

1. Check the merchant's written refund or cancellation policy.
2. Contact the merchant through an official channel when appropriate.
3. Save the case number and any promised processing timeframe.
4. Review the transaction status in your card account.
5. Follow your card provider's official dispute process if the issue remains unresolved.

## Avoid common mistakes

Do not assume that a merchant's refund approval means the credit will appear immediately. Do not submit repeated payment attempts or duplicate refund requests without checking the status. Keep communication factual and provide only the information needed to identify the transaction.

For account-specific questions, use the card provider's official support channel. You can review CBPayments at [https://www.cbpayments.cc/](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and check current terms and product details.

## FAQ

### What if the refund is taking longer than expected?
Compare the merchant's stated timeframe with the transaction status, then contact the merchant or card provider using official channels.

### What evidence is useful?
Receipts, order numbers, cancellation confirmation, merchant correspondence, transaction records, and support case numbers are useful starting points.

## Final takeaway

Keep a clear timeline, save relevant documents, and follow the official process. This makes refund and dispute questions easier to resolve.
