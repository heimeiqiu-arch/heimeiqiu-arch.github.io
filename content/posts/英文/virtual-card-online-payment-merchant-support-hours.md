Find the right official support channel and expected response hours when an online order or card payment needs clarification.

When a payment or refund needs attention, use verified support channels and keep the transaction record available. Unexpected messages that demand sensitive information or urge immediate action should be treated cautiously.

## Verify the contact route

Open the merchant's or card provider's official website or app directly. Check contact details from the account dashboard or official help centre rather than relying on a link in an unsolicited message. Support hours and response times can vary by provider and region.

## Protect payment information

1. Do not disclose full card details or one-time codes in ordinary messages.
2. Avoid links that demand urgent action or unusual fees to release a refund.
3. Check sender addresses and website domains carefully.
4. Save legitimate case numbers and support correspondence.
5. Review account activity and available transaction alerts.

## If a message looks suspicious

Do not reply or open its links. Contact the merchant or card provider using a verified route and report the message if the provider offers that option. If you suspect unauthorised activity, follow the provider's official account-security and dispute procedures.

For general information about CBPayments, visit [https://www.cbpayments.cc/](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and review the current product details and terms.

## FAQ

### Should support ever ask for a one-time code?
Do not share one-time authentication codes with a person who contacts you. Follow only the authentication flow you initiated through the official service.

### What records should I keep?
Retain transaction references, receipts, official support case numbers, and relevant correspondence securely.

## Final takeaway

Verify support contacts independently, keep sensitive information private, and use official account tools to review payment activity.
