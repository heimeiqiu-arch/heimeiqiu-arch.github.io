# Using a Virtual Card for Stock Photo and Video Subscriptions

Stock photo and video libraries may sell individual downloads, credit packs, or recurring subscriptions. A virtual card may be accepted for some plans, but payment support varies by provider, region, and card programme. For virtual card information, visit [CBPayments](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and check the current terms before paying for creative assets.

## Understand the Licence

Check whether the licence covers commercial use, client work, advertising, redistribution, or use in templates. A subscription may have download limits or rules that differ from a single asset purchase.

## Check the Subscription Model

Confirm the billing frequency, included downloads, rollover rules, renewal price, and cancellation deadline. Save the licence terms that applied when you downloaded each asset.

## Confirm Payment and Currency

Verify card acceptance, final price, taxes, and conversion terms. If the payment fails, check order status and available balance before retrying.

## Keep Proof of Purchase

Store receipts, asset identifiers, and licence records together. If an asset is missing or a subscription is billed incorrectly, contact the provider with the relevant details.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I subscribe to a stock media library with a virtual card?

Possibly, if the provider accepts the card type and region.

### Does paying guarantee commercial usage rights?

No. The licence terms determine permitted use.

### Can I get a refund for downloads?

The provider's policy and applicable law determine eligibility.

## Final Thoughts

Before paying, verify merchant acceptance, the total amount, billing frequency, cancellation and refund terms, and the card programme's current rules. Keep receipts and confirmation records, and use the merchant's official support channel when a transaction is unclear.
