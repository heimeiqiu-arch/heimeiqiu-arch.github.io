Digital asset marketplaces sell templates, fonts, icons, audio, stock elements, and other downloadable resources. A virtual card may work at checkout when the marketplace accepts it, while licensing terms determine how the asset can be used.

For a general overview of virtual-card options, visit [CBPayments](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and review the available product terms before choosing a payment method.

## Review the licence before checkout
Check whether the purchase allows commercial use, client work, redistribution, or use in products for resale. Some assets are subscription-only or require an extended licence. Save the licence receipt and product page details in case you need to confirm usage rights later.

## Keep purchases traceable
For business projects, record the asset, marketplace, project name, and invoice. If the marketplace offers a recurring membership, confirm what happens to downloaded assets after cancellation. Use a reusable card for continuing membership when supported.

## Troubleshoot payment problems
Check the total, card status, available limit, billing details, and supported payment methods. If the order is charged but the download is unavailable, check the account library before attempting another payment.

## FAQ
**Can a virtual card buy digital assets?** Often possible, but merchant acceptance varies.

**Does a receipt grant every type of usage right?** No. Follow the specific asset licence.

**Where can I explore card options?** Visit [CBPayments](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and confirm the card is suitable for the marketplace.

## Final thoughts
Keep the asset licence and payment receipt together so creative purchases remain easy to audit.
