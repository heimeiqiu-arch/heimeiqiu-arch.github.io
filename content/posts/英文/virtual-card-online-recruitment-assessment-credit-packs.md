Record prepaid assessment credits, expiry conditions, usage, and remaining balances when paying for online recruitment tests.

Online business platforms often bill by employee count, agent seats, assessment credits, conversations, or feature modules. Reviewing the pricing model before checkout helps teams avoid unexpected charges and keep renewals aligned with actual usage.

## Check the billing model

Confirm the current plan, billing currency, included quantity, overage rates, add-ons, and cancellation conditions on the provider's official website. Determine whether inactive users still count as paid seats and whether unused credits expire. Save the terms and order confirmation.

## Keep costs under control

1. Assign a service owner and budget owner.
2. Review active users or usage volume before renewal.
3. Record the renewal date and cancellation deadline.
4. Keep the invoice, receipt, and usage report.
5. Compare the bill with the agreed plan and actual activity.

## Investigate unexpected changes

Check for new users, higher volume, upgraded tiers, or optional modules. Ask the provider for an itemised explanation if the invoice does not match expectations. If checkout fails, verify whether the original transaction or order exists before trying again.

Use official checkout pages and protect payment details and verification codes. Merchant acceptance varies, so confirm supported payment methods before relying on a virtual card.

You can review CBPayments at [https://www.cbpayments.cc/](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and check current terms, fees, availability, and product information.

## FAQ

### Do unused credits always carry forward?
No. The provider's terms determine whether credits expire, roll over, or can be refunded.

### What records should be kept?
Retain invoices, receipts, usage reports, plan terms, renewal dates, and notes on any change in volume.

## Final takeaway

Know which metric drives the bill, monitor usage, and review the invoice before renewal. Clear ownership and records make software costs easier to manage.
