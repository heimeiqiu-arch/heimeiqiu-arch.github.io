Use transaction status and the merchant's guidance to avoid duplicate orders or repeated authorisation attempts.

A checkout decision may involve the merchant, payment processor, and card issuer. A decline or review does not always explain the underlying reason, so start by checking the transaction details and the provider's guidance.

## Review the transaction

Check the merchant's order history and your card account. Note the timestamp, amount, currency, merchant descriptor, and exact error message. If a review or decline appears, follow official instructions rather than repeatedly submitting the same payment.

## A safe next-step checklist

1. Confirm the merchant's official checkout.
2. Check whether an order or authorisation already exists.
3. Review card balance, transaction limits, and billing details.
4. Follow the issuer or merchant's retry guidance.
5. Save the error message and relevant reference numbers.

## Avoid duplicate activity

Repeated attempts may create multiple pending authorisations or duplicate orders. If the status is unclear, contact the merchant about the order and your card provider about authorisation. Do not share full card credentials or one-time codes with anyone contacting you unexpectedly.

Virtual card acceptance varies by merchant and provider. Confirm supported payment methods before relying on one for an urgent transaction.

## FAQ

### Should I retry immediately after a decline?
First check whether the order or transaction was created and follow the provider's official guidance.

### What information helps support?
Provide the time, amount, currency, merchant, error message, and order reference. Never send full card details or authentication codes.

## Final takeaway

Verify the status, follow official guidance, and retry only when you know the previous attempt did not complete.
