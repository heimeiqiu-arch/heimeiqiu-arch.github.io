Review marketplace commissions, payment fees, seller terms, and refund processes before buying digital game items.

Digital marketplace purchases may include platform fees, seller conditions, or restrictions based on account and region. Before paying, check exactly what will be delivered and how the platform handles disputes and refunds.

## Before checkout

Use the official marketplace and confirm the product, seller, region, total price, currency, and any commission or service fee. Read the refund policy and verify whether the item is transferable or tied permanently to an account.

## Payment checklist

1. Confirm the account and product details.
2. Review fees and refund conditions.
3. Save the listing, receipt, and order reference.
4. Check order status before retrying a failed checkout.
5. Contact the seller or marketplace through official channels if the item is missing.

Protect account credentials and payment verification codes. Not every platform accepts every virtual card, so check supported methods before relying on one.

## FAQ

### Can marketplace purchases always be refunded?
No. Eligibility depends on platform rules, item type, and whether the product has been delivered or used.

### What should I keep?
Retain the listing details, receipt, order reference, and support correspondence.

## Final takeaway

Check the seller, fees, product restrictions, and refund policy before paying. Keep records and verify order status before retrying.
