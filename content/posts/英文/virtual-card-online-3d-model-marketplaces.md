3D asset marketplaces sell models, textures, materials, and production-ready design resources. A virtual card may be accepted at checkout, while the licence determines whether an asset can be used commercially.

For a general overview of virtual-card options, visit [CBPayments](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and review the available product terms before choosing a payment method.

## Review the asset licence
Check whether the purchase permits commercial projects, client work, redistribution, modification, or inclusion in a product for sale. Some assets are personal-use only or require a separate extended licence. Keep the licence terms and receipt with the downloaded file.

## Organize project purchases
For a studio or freelancer, record the asset, marketplace, project, and invoice. If the platform offers a recurring membership, confirm download limits and what happens to previously acquired assets after cancellation. Use a reusable card for renewals where supported.

## Resolve payment problems
Check the total, card status, available limit, billing details, and platform acceptance. If payment is recorded but the file is missing, review the account library and order history before making another purchase.

## FAQ
**Can a virtual card buy 3D assets?** It depends on the marketplace’s payment rules.

**Does purchasing an asset grant unrestricted rights?** No. Follow the specific licence.

**Where can I review card options?** Visit [CBPayments](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and verify acceptance before checkout.

## Final thoughts
Keep licences and receipts attached to project records to avoid later usage disputes.
