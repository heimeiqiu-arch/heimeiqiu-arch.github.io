Learn how to verify order and transaction status before refreshing a checkout page that may have submitted a payment.

A checkout error can result from an input field, browser session, merchant system, or payment authorisation. Before trying again, confirm whether the order or transaction was created.

## Check the immediate details

Use the merchant's official website or app. Review the exact error, card expiry format, security-code field, billing address, and final currency amount. If you are using a mobile browser, check whether the page fully loaded and whether autofill inserted outdated information.

## Safe troubleshooting steps

1. Note the error message and time.
2. Check order history and the card transaction status.
3. Correct only the field identified by the merchant's error.
4. Follow official guidance before refreshing or retrying.
5. Save the order reference and receipt if successful.

## Protect your payment information

Do not share full card credentials, security codes, or one-time authentication codes with someone contacting you unexpectedly. If the status is unclear, contact the merchant to confirm the order and the card provider to check the transaction.

Virtual card acceptance varies by merchant. Confirm supported methods and any card requirements before relying on one for a time-sensitive purchase.

You can review CBPayments at [https://www.cbpayments.cc/](https://www.cbpayments.cc/). Check current product details, fees, availability, and terms before choosing a payment option.

## FAQ

### Should I refresh the page after submitting payment?
First check whether the order or transaction exists. Refreshing or retrying without checking may create duplicate activity.

### What should I save?
Keep the timestamp, error message, order reference, receipt, and transaction status, stored securely.

## Final takeaway

Identify the error, confirm transaction status, and follow official guidance before another attempt.
