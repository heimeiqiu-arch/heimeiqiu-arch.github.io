Document AI APIs extract fields, classify documents, and process forms or invoices. Cost and accuracy depend on document type, page volume, language coverage, and the need for human review.

For a general overview of virtual-card options for eligible online services, visit [CBPayments](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and review the current product terms before choosing a payment method.

## Compare features and pricing

Test representative documents and check per-page or per-request charges, rate limits, storage retention, and error handling. Set usage alerts before moving from a trial to production.

Look beyond the advertised starting price. Check billing units, usage allowances, additional-user costs, taxes, renewal frequency, and trial-conversion terms. If demand can grow, estimate likely usage and understand how overages are calculated before committing.

## Check payment compatibility

Before checkout, confirm the provider's accepted card types, billing-country requirements, and verification steps. Merchant acceptance and issuer restrictions vary, so a virtual card should not be assumed to work with every service. If a transaction is declined, read the error message, check the issuer's transaction status, and verify billing details before retrying.

## Keep renewals and records organised

Assign an owner to the service, record the next renewal date, and review seats or usage before the billing cycle ends. Save invoices and approval records with the relevant project or department budget. Plan payment-method changes early if the provider may require additional verification.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can this type of service be paid for with a virtual card?** It may be possible if the vendor accepts the card type and the issuer permits the transaction. Confirm directly with the provider.

**Does a virtual card guarantee approval or uninterrupted access?** No. Billing details, available balance, merchant rules, verification, account status, and contract terms still apply.

**What should I check before subscribing?** Compare the service's features, pricing basis, cancellation policy, data handling, and payment requirements. Start with a plan that fits expected usage rather than buying excess capacity.

## Final thoughts

Choose the service based on its features, security, and business fit, then confirm payment compatibility separately. Review available virtual-card options at [CBPayments](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and read the current terms before use.
