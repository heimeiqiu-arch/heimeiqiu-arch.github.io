Human translation review services may bill by word count, page, language pair, or revision round. A virtual card may be accepted for online payment, but scope and quality expectations should be agreed before work begins.

## Define the review task
Clarify whether the reviewer checks grammar, terminology, cultural tone, SEO phrasing, or technical accuracy. Agree on the file format, turnaround time, revision rounds, and how comments will be delivered. For regulated or legal content, confirm the reviewer’s relevant qualifications.

## Keep project documentation
Save the quote, source files, reviewer details, invoice, and final approved version. For ongoing projects, use a reusable card if recurring billing is supported and track costs by language and page. Avoid sending sensitive material without checking confidentiality terms.

## Resolve payment problems
Check card status, available funds, billing details, and provider acceptance. If the payment is recorded but the review has not started, contact the service through official channels with the order reference.
