Check coverage exclusions, eligible cancellation reasons, claim deadlines, and the separate cost of travel protection.

Travel add-ons and activities may have separate prices, strict deadlines, and limited refund options. Review the details before paying so you know what you are purchasing and what happens if your itinerary changes.

## Before checkout

Use the operator or booking provider's official website. Confirm the location, service date, currency, restrictions, cancellation terms, and extra charges. Save the confirmation after checkout.

## Payment checklist

1. Verify the provider and exact service.
2. Check whether the fee applies per person or per booking.
3. Read refund and change conditions.
4. Save the receipt and booking reference.
5. Compare the final transaction with the amount displayed at checkout.

## If plans change

Review the provider's rules before paying for a replacement or modification. If checkout fails, verify whether the original booking and transaction were created before retrying. Contact the travel provider for itinerary or refund questions and your card provider for transaction-status concerns.

Protect card credentials and authentication codes. Some travel merchants impose specific card requirements, so check supported methods before relying on a virtual card.

You can review CBPayments at [https://www.cbpayments.cc/](https://www.cbpayments.cc/). Check current fees, product details, availability, and terms before selecting a payment method.

## FAQ

### Are travel add-ons always refundable?
No. Refundability depends on the provider's policy, the type of add-on, and when a change is requested.

### What should I save?
Keep the itinerary, service details, receipt, booking reference, policy terms, and messages about changes or refunds.

## Final takeaway

Confirm exactly what the fee covers, understand the restrictions, and keep booking records to help resolve discrepancies later.
