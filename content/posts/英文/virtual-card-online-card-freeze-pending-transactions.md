Understand that freezing a card may not cancel authorisations or previously initiated transactions.

Card account settings and transaction notifications can help you keep track of online activity, but they do not replace checking the transaction history or following the provider's official security guidance.

## Review account activity

Check the transaction date, amount, currency, merchant descriptor, and status. Small verification amounts, pending authorisations, or delayed notifications can appear differently depending on the merchant and provider. Do not assume an unfamiliar charge is harmless; investigate it if you cannot connect it to a purchase.

## Security checklist

1. Use the official provider app or website.
2. Protect your password and authentication methods.
3. Review active sessions and sign out of shared devices.
4. Check saved recurring payments after replacing card details.
5. Contact official support about suspicious activity or unclear pending transactions.

## Understand card controls

Freezing or replacing a card may not cancel a transaction already authorised, and recurring merchants may need updated payment details. Review the provider's explanation before relying on a setting to stop a specific charge.

Never share full card credentials or one-time codes with someone who contacts you unexpectedly. Save transaction references and follow the official dispute process if activity appears unauthorised.

For general information about CBPayments, visit [https://www.cbpayments.cc/](https://www.cbpayments.cc/) and review the current product details and terms.

## FAQ

### Does freezing a card cancel pending transactions?
Not necessarily. Existing authorisations may still settle or be released according to the merchant and provider's processes.

### What should I do after replacing card details?
Review account activity, update legitimate recurring payments where needed, and check for unfamiliar transactions.

## Final takeaway

Use account controls alongside transaction reviews, protect credentials, and confirm how the provider handles pending activity.
