SEO audit software crawls websites to identify technical issues such as broken links, missing metadata, and redirect chains. Plans may limit crawl URLs, projects, or scheduled audits. A virtual card can help track the subscription when accepted by the provider.

## Compare the billing model before checkout

Check crawl limits, JavaScript rendering, scheduled audits, export formats, and whether multiple domains are included. Estimate site size before choosing a tier, and distinguish one-off crawl needs from ongoing monitoring.

## Troubleshoot a declined payment methodically

If payment fails, verify balance, transaction limits, billing details, recurring-payment eligibility, and authentication prompts. Confirm the provider accepts the card type and region. For unexpected charges, compare crawl volume and project counts with the invoice.

## Keep recurring costs under control

Remove test domains and unused projects before renewal. Keep audit exports and invoices with website operations records. Before cancelling, save reports and document important crawl settings so future audits remain comparable.

## Frequently asked questions

Are SEO audits always one-time purchases? No; many tools use subscriptions. Can a virtual card pay for audit software? Only if the vendor accepts it.
